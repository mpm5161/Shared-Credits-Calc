💬 Discovery questions — Creative Cloud ▼
Usage and workflow
1.What types of creative assets does your team produce most often right now — images, video, or a mix?
2.How many people in your organization are actively creating content today, and what tools are they using?
3.Walk me through what a typical content production cycle looks like for your team — from brief to final asset.
4.Where does AI generation fit today, if at all? Are teams experimenting with any tools independently?
Volume and frequency
5.How many assets does your team typically produce in a month — rough order of magnitude is fine?
6.Are there seasonal spikes in creative output, like campaign launches or product releases, that would change usage significantly?
7.How many iterations does a typical asset go through before it is approved?
Video specifically
8.Is video generation something your team is actively doing, or is that further out on the roadmap?
9.What formats are you targeting — short-form social, long-form, internal communications?
10.What resolution and quality standard does your brand require for video output?
Sizing and scale
11.How do you expect AI-assisted creation to change the volume of assets your team produces over the next year?
12.Is this a single team use case or are there multiple departments that would draw from the same credit pool?

💬 Discovery questions — Document Cloud ▼
Document landscape
1.What types of documents does your organization work with most — contracts, reports, forms, technical docs, or something else?
2.Where do these documents live today — shared drives, document management systems, email, or scattered across tools?
3.How many documents would you estimate are actively referenced by your team in a given month?
4.Are there specific document types that are high-value — ones where finding the right answer quickly would save meaningful time?
Knowledge Base sizing
5.Which teams or roles would be querying these documents most often?
6.How often does a typical user need to search or ask questions across your document library in a given day or week?
7.How many users would need access to Knowledge Base at launch versus over the first year?
8.Are most of your users on Acrobat Studio, or are there users on other plans like Reader, Standard, or Express?
Analyzer sizing
9.Are there processes today where your team manually extracts data from documents — like pulling fields from contracts or tables from reports?
10.How many documents would go through that extraction process in a typical month?
11.How many specific data points or attributes does your team need to pull from each document on average?
12.Are the documents fairly consistent in structure, or do they vary a lot across sources?
Setup and rollout
13.How quickly are you looking to have this operational — are there internal deadlines or project timelines driving the rollout?
14.Who internally would own the initial document ingestion and configuration process?
15.Are there compliance or data residency requirements that would affect which documents can be ingested?
